TRIOUS PHARMA WEBSITE
Files:
- index.html: homepage
- privacy.html: privacy policy
- terms.html: terms and conditions
- images/: website image assets

PUBLISHING ON HOSTINGER:
1. Log in to Hostinger and open Websites.
2. Select triousphrama.com, then Manage.
3. Open Files > File Manager.
4. Open the public_html folder.
5. Upload the ZIP contents (not the outer folder) into public_html.
6. Confirm index.html is directly inside public_html.
7. Visit https://triousphrama.com

Before publishing:
- Confirm that info@triousphrama.com is an active inbox and can receive mail.
- Replace generic business wording with accurate details about the real business.
- Only list products and regulatory claims that are accurate and authorized.
- Product images are illustrative, not product listings.
